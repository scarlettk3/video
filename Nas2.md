Analyses the effect of various changes in early human society from nomadism to settled life and early civilisation (such as, the emergence of agriculture, changes in food habits, basic technologies like construction, transport, pottery, metallurgy), changes in human habitation, family structures and relationships, the nature of work, people’s socio-cultural beliefs and concepts over time (e.g., Ahimsa, and the fallout of major wars or invasions) that significantly impacted human societies
